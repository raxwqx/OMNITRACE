ANALYST LAB / 03
Analyst Lab
Ten local-first utilities for evidence preparation, indicator extraction and repeatable analysis. Processing stays in your browser unless a tool explicitly says otherwise.

        

          
01 / IOC Extractor
Extract likely IPs, domains, URLs, emails and hashes from pasted text.

          
02 / Defang & Refang
Make indicators safer to share, or restore their standard spelling.

          
03 / Base64 utility
Encode or decode text locally using UTF-8 support.

          
04 / URL codec
Encode or decode URL components without visiting a URL.

          
05 / SHA-256 text digest
Calculate a SHA-256 digest of text locally. This does not hash a file.

          
06 / Timestamp converter
Convert Unix seconds or milliseconds into a readable UTC date.

          
07 / IPv4 CIDR calculator
Calculate network, broadcast and usable host count locally.

          
08 / Source assessment
A transparent checklist to assess source quality, not a truth detector.

          
09 / Relationship mapper
Record a simple lead relationship in a readable, exportable format.

          
10 / Quick report builder
Create a timestamped research note and export it as a text report.

        

        
These helpers run locally in the browser. Extracted indicators and relationship notes are leads, not verified facts. Avoid pasting passwords, private records or sensitive personal data.
